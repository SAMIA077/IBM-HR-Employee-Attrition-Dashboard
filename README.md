# IBM-HR-Employee-Attrition-Dashboard
HR dashboard — "IBM Employee Attrition," identifying key drivers of employee turnover
IBM HR Employee Attrition Dashboard (Power BI)

An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing the IBM HR Employee Attrition dataset to identify the key drivers of employee turnover.

Overview

The report helps HR teams answer a critical question: why are employees leaving, and who is most at risk? It turns raw HR data into clear, decision-ready visuals.

Pages


Overview – High-level KPIs including total employees and attrition metrics, with cards and summary visuals
Page 1 – Supporting breakdowns and detailed views


Focus Areas


Total Employees and overall attrition rate
Key drivers of employee turnover
Demographic and job-related attrition patterns


Tech Stack


Power BI Desktop (.pbix)
Data model with measures (e.g. Total Employees)
Custom theme and styled card visuals


How to Use


Install Power BI Desktop (free from Microsoft).
Open IBM_POwer_Bi_Project.pbix.
Interact with the visuals and slicers to explore attrition drivers.


Key Learnings


Building HR analytics KPIs and measures in Power BI
Designing a clean, themed dashboard layout
Translating workforce data into retention insights
